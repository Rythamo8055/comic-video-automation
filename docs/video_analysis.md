# Reference Video Technical Analysis

**Title:** *This Omnitrix Summons Alien BADDIES! | Chaquetrix Pt.1*  
**Duration:** 519.9 seconds (~8 minutes 40 seconds)  
**Resolution:** 1280x720 @ 23.98 fps (H.264 / AAC 44.1 kHz Stereo)  

---

## 1. Video Cuts & Editorial Rhythm

- **Cut Frequency & Duration:**
  - **Average cut duration:** `2.20 seconds`
  - **Scene count in first 120s:** 56 cuts
  - **Shortest cuts:** `0.33s` (quick dramatic reactions, impact punchlines)
  - **Longest cuts:** `10.8s` (multi-panel page sweeps or establishing context)
- **Cut Types:**
  - **Straight Hard Cuts (~95%):** Almost all transitions are sharp, instant cuts. There are virtually no crossfades or wipes. This maintains high viewer retention and comic-book tempo.
  - **Sentence Synchronization:** Every cut is timed to sentence breaks, audio commas, or narrator pauses.
  - **Punchline Jump Cuts:** Instant zoom-in ($1.3\times - 1.5\times$) on a character's face when an unexpected or humorous line is spoken.

---

## 2. Transformations & Camera Motion

- **Ken Burns Vertical Pan (Top-to-Bottom):**
  - Used for standard comic pages and tall panels.
  - The camera starts focused on the top panel/speech bubble and moves vertically downward at a constant velocity to guide reading order before cutting to the next frame.
- **Continuous Push-In (Slow Zoom):**
  - Static panels are animated with a continuous slow scale ($1.0\times \to 1.15\times$ over 2–4 seconds).
  - Keeps 2D artwork visually dynamic without causing motion sickness.
- **Screen Shake / Impact Shock:**
  - Fast 3–5 frame directional offset ($x \pm 10\text{px}, y \pm 10\text{px}$) triggered on explosions, punches, or dramatic sound effects.
- **Letterboxing & Viewport Fitting:**
  - YouTube standard is 16:9. Comic panels with non-16:9 aspect ratios are framed cleanly with black borders or cropped dynamically around the focal subject.

---

## 3. Audio Engineering & Voice Delivery

- **Narration Pacing:**
  - **Total words:** ~1,500 words
  - **Speaking rate:** `172.8 Words Per Minute (WPM)`
  - Energetic, punchy, conversational style typical of anime/comic recap channels (Comicstorian, Manhwa recaps).
- **Loudness & Dynamics:**
  - **Voice Track:** Mean volume `-12.8 dB`, peak `0.0 dB`. Sits clearly in front of the mix.
  - **Background Music (BGM):** Continuous upbeat instrumental running underneath at `-16 dB to -18 dB`.
  - **Sidechain Ducking:** BGM automatically drops by 3–4 dB during spoken sentences and rises during scene pauses.
  - **Sound Effects (SFX):** Whooshes on scene cuts, energy hums, and impact thuds on action panels.
